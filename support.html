Is the forecast live?

No. It is built from the workouts members plan, so it shows when a gym is likely to be busy, not how busy it is right now.

Why does my gym say "No forecast yet"?

A forecast appears once 20 members of the gym count their plan in it. Invite people who train there.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. Your plans and data are removed and you leave every forecast.

How do I report someone?

Open your gym’s split cards and choose Report on their card. We review reports within 24 hours.
